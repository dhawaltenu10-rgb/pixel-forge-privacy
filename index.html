Privacy Policy for PIXEL FORGE PRO

Last updated: November 26, 2026

PIXEL FORGE PRO ("we", "our", or "the app") is an offline image resizing and format conversion utility. We respect your privacy and keep your media safe.

1. Data Collection

PIXEL FORGE PRO does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information, account credentials, location data, or usage analytics on external servers.

2. Local Photo Processing

All photo resizing, cropping, compression, and format conversion (JPG, PNG, WEBP) happen 100% locally on your device. Converted files are saved to your device gallery (Pictures/PhotoResizer) and local app history, and are never uploaded to any cloud server.

3. Permissions

The app only accesses media storage needed for selecting images and saving converted photos to your device.

4. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time on this page.

5. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, reach out via our developer profile.
